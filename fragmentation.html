Project 04 · Network Metrics

Fragmentation is not a single number.

The work separates structural political fragmentation into three complementary dimensions: separation strength, effective number of fragments, and branching across scales. Adjust the toy system below to see why each dimension matters.

This lab is an explanatory toy visualization; slider values are not empirical country estimates.

Three dimensions

1. Separation strengthHow strongly ties concentrate within rather than between communities. The paper recommends the pairwise adaptive E–I index for this role.
2. Number of fragmentsThe effective number of communities (ENC), which accounts for unequal community sizes.
3. Between-level branchingThe Effective Branching Factor (EBF), introduced to quantify how communities split across multiscale levels.
