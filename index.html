About Me

I obtained my Ph.D. degree at the University of Zurich in 2026, advised by Prof. Frank Esser, Prof. Laia Castro Herrero, and Prof. Alexandre Bovet. I was a visiting scholar at the University of Pennsylvania for one semester in 2025, hosted by Prof. Sandra González-Bailón. My research interests lie at the intersection of social science theories and computational methods. I am interested both in developing computational models to understand complex real-world social phenomena and in using social theories to guide the development of socially beneficial technologies. My current research also focuses on multi-agent AI alignment and governance, with an enthusiasm for contributing to a world that is both technologically efficient and socially tolerant. Please feel free to contact me if you are interested in collaboration or simply in having a conversation.
